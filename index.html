Meridian

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 17, 2026  ·  Last Updated: April 17, 2026

The short version: Meridian stores all of your health and fitness data on your device only. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not send it to any server. Your data stays with you.

1. Who We Are

Meridian is an all-in-one fitness tracking application for iOS developed by Kyle Feller ("we," "us," or "our"). Meridian helps you unify health metrics from your wearable devices — including Apple Watch, Garmin, Whoop, and Oura Ring — alongside manual tracking for nutrition, workouts, sleep, and body metrics.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the address listed in Section 10.

2. Information We Collect

Meridian collects information in two ways: data you provide directly, and health data you authorize from Apple Health or connected wearable devices.

Information You Provide Directly

  • Profile information: Your name, age, height, body weight, activity level, and fitness goal (entered during onboarding)
  • Nutrition logs: Meal names, calorie counts, and macro values (protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber) you log each day
  • Workout logs: Workout type (run, lift, or other), duration, intensity, exercises, sets, reps, weights, distance, and pace
  • Body metrics: Daily body weight entries you log manually
  • Manual health entries: Steps, sleep times, sleep quality ratings, and water intake you enter when no wearable is connected
  • Custom foods and meal presets: Food items and meal combinations you save for reuse
  • Account credentials: Email address and password if you create an account (stored securely — see Section 4)

Health Data from Apple Health and Wearables

With your explicit permission, Meridian reads the following data from Apple Health and connected wearable devices (Garmin, Whoop, Oura Ring):

  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Daily step count
  • Sleep data (duration, stages, and device-generated sleep scores)
  • Active calories and active minutes
  • Distance traveled
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2), where available
  • Recovery scores (Whoop Recovery %, Oura Readiness Score, Garmin Body Battery)
  • Workout sessions previously recorded by your wearable

You control which data sources Meridian can access. You may revoke access at any time through the Settings app on your iPhone (Settings → Privacy & Security → Health).

3. How We Use Your Information

All information collected by Meridian is used solely to provide and improve the app's core features:

  • Displaying your daily health metrics on the Dashboard
  • Calculating your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) and personalized calorie and macro targets
  • Tracking your workout history, personal records, and training trends
  • Generating progress charts and analytics (weight trend, strength progress, cardio history, nutrition averages)
  • Sending local push notifications for workout reminders, meal logging reminders, and streak alerts — these are sent by your device, not by our servers
  • Writing completed workouts back to Apple Health (with your permission) to keep your health record accurate

Not Medical Advice: Meridian displays health metrics for informational and fitness tracking purposes only. The information provided by Meridian — including heart rate, HRV, sleep scores, recovery scores, and calorie estimates — is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions about your health.

4. How We Store Your Information

Phase 1 (Current): All data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage, a standard React Native local storage library. Your data never leaves your device. There is no Meridian server, no cloud database, and no backend that receives or processes your information.

Because data is stored locally, it is tied to your device. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data. We strongly recommend not using Meridian as your sole record of health data — your wearable's companion app (Garmin Connect, Whoop, Oura, Apple Health) maintains its own history.

Phase 2 (Planned — Cloud Sync): A future version of Meridian will introduce optional cloud sync via Supabase, enabling multi-device access and data backup. When this feature is released, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect what data is transmitted, how it is secured (AES-256 encryption in transit and at rest), your rights under GDPR and CCPA, and how to request deletion of your account and data. Cloud sync will be opt-in.

5. Third-Party Services and Wearable Integrations

Meridian integrates with the following third-party platforms to read your health data. Each platform has its own privacy practices:

  • Apple Health (HealthKit): Data accessed with your explicit permission through Apple's HealthKit framework. Apple's Privacy Policy governs Apple's data handling.
  • Whoop: If you connect your Whoop account, Meridian requests permission to access your recovery score, sleep performance, HRV, workout strain, and body measurements. This data is fetched directly from the Whoop API using OAuth 2.0 and stored on your device. Meridian does not share your Whoop data with any third party. Your Whoop access token is stored in device storage (unencrypted in the current version; encrypted storage is planned for a future update). You can disconnect Whoop at any time from the Settings screen, which removes the stored tokens from your device. Whoop's Privacy Policy governs how Whoop stores and processes your data on their end. Meridian does not receive or store your Whoop credentials — only the OAuth access token issued by Whoop.
  • Oura Ring: Connected via a personal access token you generate and paste into Meridian. Oura's Privacy Policy governs Oura's data handling.
  • Garmin: Connected via Garmin's OAuth API (integration in development). Garmin's Privacy Policy governs Garmin's data handling.
  • Open Food Facts: Meridian queries the Open Food Facts food database API to provide food search results in the Calorie Tracker. Your food search queries are sent to the Open Food Facts API but are not linked to your identity. Open Food Facts is a free, open, collaborative food products database.

Meridian does not share your personal data with any of these third parties beyond the connection you authorize. The integrations are read-only (or write-only for Apple Health workout write-back) and scoped to the minimum data necessary.

6. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • We do not use any third-party analytics SDK (no Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar)
  • We do not collect precise location data
  • We do not share or sell your data to any third party for advertising or commercial purposes
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not collect payment information (the app is currently free)

7. Children's Privacy

Meridian is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete that information.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Because all data in the current version of Meridian is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times:

  • Access: All of your data is visible within the app at all times
  • Deletion: Uninstalling Meridian from your device permanently deletes all locally stored data
  • Revoke wearable access: You may disconnect any wearable at any time via the Devices section in Settings, or through your iPhone's system Settings → Privacy & Security → Health
  • California residents (CCPA): Meridian does not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • European residents (GDPR): Your legal basis for processing is your explicit consent, given when you connect a wearable or create an account. You may withdraw consent at any time by disconnecting the data source or deleting the app. When cloud sync is introduced in Phase 2, a formal data deletion request mechanism will be provided.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you within the app. Your continued use of Meridian after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Significant planned changes that will require policy updates: introduction of cloud sync (Phase 2), AI-powered insights that send health data to external APIs (Phase 3), and any introduction of paid features.
